# Source Scripts

Speaker policy: **English = Australian officer/service professional; Cantonese = immigrant/community client.**

## D001 — Applying for an ABN
Topic: Business

**S1 · Officer / professional · English → Cantonese:** Good morning. You’re speaking with a small-business adviser. I’m calling about a new sole-trader registration. I’ve found the enquiry. Before we get into the details, tell me what you need resolved.

**S2 · Immigrant / client · Cantonese → English:** 而家我最憂心係可能要延遲開業或者營運。我想喺下星期五之前處理好。

**S3 · Officer / professional · English → Cantonese:** What we need to look at is Australian Business Number, or ABN. official business details must match the entity actually trading. I’ll check the details on your case now.

**S4 · Immigrant / client · Cantonese → English:** 我份文件寫住計劃九月一日開始營業。呢個資料我之前記低咗，但唔肯定而家仲係咪最新。你可唔可以幫我核實？

**S5 · Officer / professional · English → Cantonese:** The record matches that detail. Next, submit the correct application or update and keep the confirmation. Check every date and number before submitting.

**S6 · Immigrant / client · Cantonese → English:** 我已經準備咗有相身份證明同稅務資料。其中一份係電子版本。你哋要正本，定係清晰上載副本都可以？

**S7 · Officer / professional · English → Cantonese:** Unless your notice says otherwise, use the business online portal. Upload photo ID and tax details, then save the receipt or reference.

**S8 · Immigrant / client · Cantonese → English:** 通常要等幾耐？我擔心可能要延遲開業或者營運。如果聽日都冇消息，我係咪應該再打電話？

**S9 · Officer / professional · English → Cantonese:** Allow about five business days. Meanwhile, keep using only verified business details until any change is confirmed. Don’t submit the same request twice unless asked.

**S10 · Immigrant / client · Cantonese → English:** 如果我開咗第一張單之後 ABN 先批落嚟，我係咪要重新開過張單？

**S11 · Officer / professional · English → Cantonese:** If the tax details on the invoice change, update the customer in writing and keep the corrected record with your accounts.

**S12 · Immigrant / client · Cantonese → English:** 好，我今日就處理，啲文件亦會放埋一齊。

**S13 · Officer / professional · English → Cantonese:** Fine, I’ll do it that way and keep proof of the date I sent it.
