# Official Reference Notes

Checked for this practice pack on **17 August 2026**. Rules, fees, eligibility and service procedures can change, so verify the live official source before your actual CCL test or before treating a practice scenario as real-world advice.

## NAATI CCL

Use the current NAATI CCL test page and Candidate Instructions as the source of truth. The pack is designed around the current structure: two approximately 300-word dialogues, short segments of no more than 35 words, a chime before the candidate interprets, Australian community topics, and current repeat/timing rules.

Official sites to check:

## Australian terminology used in this pack

The vocabulary and scenarios use terminology from Australian public bodies and services including Fair Work Ombudsman, Services Australia/Centrelink, Department of Home Affairs, healthdirect, the ATO, ACCC, AFCA, NDIS, the Commonwealth Ombudsman and state/territory tenancy bodies. Jurisdiction-specific procedures can differ.

## Important limitation

The dialogues are **original study simulations**, not leaked or reproduced NAATI test items. The generated MP3 files are **synthetic speech recordings**. They are independently produced and are not NAATI recordings.
